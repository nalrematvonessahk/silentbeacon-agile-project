## Updating risks

At each actual Daily Scrum or planning session, record new blockers and changed risk conditions. At Review, link product evidence and decide whether to change scope. Record the date, decision maker, trigger, action, and follow-up date. Close a risk only with evidence that its condition has been resolved; writing a mitigation does not close it.

## Change control

Use the [decision log](decision-log.md) for decisions and [change log](change-log.md) for changes to dates, scope, acceptance interpretation, or estimates.
