# Team communication plan

Prepared October 29, 2026. This is a proposed routine and a set of unsent templates. No Telegram messages or Google Meet meetings are claimed as sent, scheduled, or held.

## Channels and responsibilities

| Channel | Use | Record to retain |
|---|---|---|
| Telegram team chat | Daily coordination, blockers, short clarifications | Actual message link or a dated agreed summary, where sharing is permitted |
| Google Meet or an in-person meeting | Planning, Review, Retrospective and complex decisions | Actual agenda, date, attendees, notes and decisions; recording only with consent |
| Trello | Ordered backlog, selected sprint work, task status and evidence links | Current cards and genuine activity history |
| Figma | Editable web designs, prototypes and design review | Actual file/node/version links and review outcomes |
| GitHub | Documentation, changes and code review where applicable | Real repository, commits, pull requests and reviewer records |

Askhat facilitates the process; Aldiyar clarifies priorities; Developers select and update their work. All five communicate availability and impediments. Select a daily meeting time together in the team's local time zone, UTC+05:00. No time or recurring meeting is currently booked.

## Daily Scrum — 10 minutes

Focus on progress toward the Sprint Goal and the next coordinated work. Each person briefly states the evidence produced since the previous check, the next task, and any blocker or collaboration needed. Inspect stalled work and WIP limits; take detailed problem solving after the Scrum.

Daily coordination is proposed across each three-week sprint; meeting dates and actual working days require team agreement. No meetings are claimed as held.

### Unsent Telegram template

```text
SilentBeacon Daily Scrum — [actual date/time]
Sprint / goal:
Since last check: [artifact or card link; actual progress only]
Next work:
Blocker / help needed:
Availability change:
Coordination needed after Scrum:
```

## Sprint Planning — 45 minutes

1. Product goal, constraints and ordered backlog — 8 minutes.
2. Each member's real availability, coordination time and contingency — 7 minutes.
3. Agree Sprint Goal and realistic story/task forecast — 15 minutes.
4. Clarify acceptance examples, dependencies and proposed task contacts — 10 minutes.
5. Confirm evidence expectations, next steps and unresolved decisions — 5 minutes.

Output: a goal, selected work, task plan, capacity record and open blockers. Source sprint assignment is not the same as actual selection. No planning meeting is invented or backdated.

## Sprint Review and Retrospective

Use the [30-minute Review agenda](sprint-reviews.md) to inspect the product and adapt the backlog. Use the [30-minute Retrospective agenda](retrospectives.md) to choose one process improvement. Preserve them as separate events even if conducted consecutively. Source refinement allocation is 30 minutes per sprint; adapt the routine through explicit team agreement.

## Decision and blocker handling

Copy consequential decisions from actual conversation into the [decision log](decision-log.md), with the real date, decision maker, rationale and affected IDs. Discuss work waiting more than one working day at the next Daily Scrum. Askhat helps surface the impediment; the relevant owner proposes a next action. No response-time promise is assumed without team agreement.
