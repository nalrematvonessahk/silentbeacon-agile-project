## Agenda — 30 minutes

1. Set the purpose and review any previous improvement action — 3 minutes.
2. Gather concrete experiences: what helped, what slowed delivery, and what was unclear — 8 minutes.
3. Discuss causes using examples rather than blame — 8 minutes.
4. Choose one feasible improvement, accepted owner and observable success measure — 8 minutes.
5. Confirm follow-up date and record — 3 minutes.

Possible prompts include work waiting more than one day, handoff clarity, evidence quality, access problems, and whether the forecast matched real availability. These are prompts, not findings.
