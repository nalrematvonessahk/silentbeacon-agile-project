## Agenda — 30 minutes

1. Revisit the Sprint Goal and actual forecast changes — 3 minutes.
2. Demonstrate the actual increment, naming simulations and unfinished criteria — 12 minutes.
3. Inspect verification evidence, questions and observed user feedback — 8 minutes.
4. Agree backlog adaptations and unresolved risks — 5 minutes.
5. Confirm recorded decisions and next actions — 2 minutes.

The source expects student observation; participants are invited only if available and authorized. An internal demonstration is not a user study. Do not report source targets such as five-user trials or ten-user comparisons as completed without records.
